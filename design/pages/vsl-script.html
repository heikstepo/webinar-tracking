1

Offer

  • Lay out the entire offer statement in the first few seconds: who it’s for, what result they get and timeframe
  • Open Loop what’s coming (how it works, who it’s for, what it costs) so they have a reason to keep watching
2

Proof

  • Right after the big claim, show why they should believe you, otherwise the promise just sounds like hype
  • Your own results first so you come across as the expert, then client results so it’s not just you
  • Always specific numbers and timeframes, since vague proof is basically no proof
3

Soft CTA

  • Give people who are already sold a way to book right away instead of forcing them through the whole video
  • Everyone else gets permission to keep watching, so nobody feels pressured and they stay on
  • You catch the hot leads early and keep the cold ones around
4

Current State + Reframe

  • Describe their situation so well they think “that’s literally me”, which builds trust fast
  • Call out what they’ve already tried and why it didn’t work, so they don’t think you’re pitching the same thing
  • Reframe it so the problem is the method, which opens the door to your Unique Mechanism
5

How It Works

  • Quickly Explain your unique mechanism tied to the service deliverables
  • Explain the actual deliverables
  • Make it clear what they do vs what you do, and keep their part as small as possible
  • Future Pace to onboarding - Walk through exactly what happens after they sign up, step by step
6

What It Costs

  • Be upfront about price instead of hiding it, so the people who book are already okay with it
  • Compare the cost to what the result is worth so the price feels small next to it
  • (Optional: Take the risk away with a guarantee or some kind of safety net, so the decision feels safe)
7

Who It’s For

  • Disqualify first, saying who it’s NOT for, so bad-fit leads filter themselves out before booking
  • Then describe the ideal client clearly, so the right people feel like it was made for them
  • Makes the offer feel exclusive instead of desperate
8

CTA

  • Tell them exactly what happens after they click (form, call, next steps) so there’s no uncertainty
  • Keep it no-pressure, “if it’s not a fit I’ll tell you”, which makes booking feel low-risk
  • Repeat the main promise from the opening so it’s the last thing in their head when they decide
