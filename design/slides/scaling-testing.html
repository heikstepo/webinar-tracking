Scaling & Testing

01

Spend

What Meta chooses.

  • In Meta, spend is earned.
  • The ad getting the money is the one meeting the platform's needs best.
  • Most spend = winning the auction, not the best CPA.

02

CPM

Cost and quality of the people you're reaching.

  • Low CPM = broad, cold, top of funnel.
  • High CPM = premium audience, or a weaker ad.

03

Frequency

Where in the funnel?

  • How often people see the ad — how the algorithm reads it.
  • Low daily frequency = prospecting.
  • High daily frequency = retargeting.

04

Cost per result

The efficiency metric.

  • Is it doing its job?
  • How well is your ad actually doing?
