How to decrease CPMs

1

Type of person / competition

More advertisers competing for the same people = more expensive auctions = higher CPM

2

Format diversity / native formats

More feed-native creative can improve predicted response + ad quality, helping you win impressions more efficiently

3

More organic content

Creates more engaged/warm users Meta can still reach inside broad targeting, which can improve delivery efficiency

4

Keep audience broad

Gives Meta access to more auctions instead of forcing delivery into a small, expensive pool

5

Better copy

Makes the right people more likely to react or convert, so Meta can show the ad more efficiently.
