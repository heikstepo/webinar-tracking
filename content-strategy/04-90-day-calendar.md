# The 90-Day Calendar

24 videos, 2 per week (**Tuesday + Saturday**), 12 weeks.

Every title is written for Rauf's actual IP — Time Based Ranges, the London/NY/Asia
sessions, Apex/Lucid — and follows the formula in `03-content-system.md`:
**named system + simplicity claim + one number.**

`$XXX,XXX` = the cumulative proof number from §1 of the content system. Fill it in once
and escalate it. **Every number in a published title must be documented.**

**Format key:** 🔧 Mechanism · 💰 Payout Recap (serial) · 🧠 Belief-Shift · 🎓 Student Proof

## Review gates

**Day 30** — Is the funnel live and attributing? Are applications arriving from
`utm_source=youtube`? If zero, the problem is the page or the CTA, not the content.

**Day 60** — Rank all videos by **applications**, not views. Kill the formats producing
none, double the ones producing them.

**Day 90** — Full review against the targets in `05-metrics.md`. Rebuild the next
quarter's calendar from measured per-video revenue rather than from this template.
