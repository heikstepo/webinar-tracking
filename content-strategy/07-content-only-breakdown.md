## 1. The one-line answer

Rauf's funnel-stage mix is **inverted**. He publishes 2.1x too much middle-of-funnel and
almost no bottom-of-funnel, at half the volume — while JJ Simon and the operator, on two
independent channels, sit within one percentage point of each other on the same split.

| Channel | TOF | MOF | **BOF** | Videos/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **JJ Simon** | 40.5% | 27.0% | **32.4%** | **10.3** |
| **Hanck (operator)** | 42.1% | 26.3% | **31.6%** | 5.7 |
| **DayTradingRauf** | 40.7% | **57.6%** | **1.7%** | 5.3 |

JJ and Hanck are effectively identical — ~41 / ~27 / ~32. Two channels in different niches
converging that tightly is a system, not a coincidence.

**Rauf's TOF is correct.** The entire problem sits downstream of it: everything that should
be BOF has been published as MOF instead.

- **MOF over-produced by 2.1x** (57.6% vs 27%)
- **BOF under-produced by 19x** (1.7% vs 32%) — that is *one video out of 59*
- **Volume at half** (5.3/mo vs 10.3/mo)

## 2. What TOF / MOF / BOF actually mean in this niche

Generic funnel definitions are useless here. This is what each stage looks like in trading
content specifically, taken from the three catalogs.

### TOF — problem-aware, no mechanism required

The viewer doesn't know your system and doesn't care yet. They want money, funding, or an
opinion. **Job: reach and subscriber capture.**

| Channel | Example | Views |
|---|---|---|
| JJ | "I made $1.5M prop firm trading and learned this" | 119,000 |
| JJ | "If You Don't Understand Prop Firms, You Don't Understand Trading" | 44,000 |
| JJ | "How To Make $10,000/Month Trading Prop Firms EASILY" | 34,000 |
| Rauf | "Top 5 Futures Prop Firms of 2025 – Instant Funding, Fast Payouts" | 25,000 |
| Rauf | "This Is WHY You Always Enter Too Early" | 12,000 |

Sub-types in use: **income aspiration** ("How To Make $X/Month"), **rankings** ("I Ranked
Every Futures Prop Firm"), **belief-shift essays** ("If You Don't Understand…"),
**origin story** ("dont give up on trading… my story" — 30,000).

### MOF — solution-aware, the mechanism itself

The viewer now wants to know *how*. Strategy reveals, system breakdowns, backtests.
**Job: prove competence.**

| Channel | Example | Views |
|---|---|---|
| JJ | "The EXACT Trading Strategy That Made Me $1,200,000" | 89,000 |
| JJ | "My $1,300,000 Trading Strategy (Explained in 10 Minutes)" | 40,000 |
| Rauf | "This Asia Session Strategy is Boring but It Makes Money" | 45,000 |
| Rauf | "I've Been Hiding The Full Time Based Ranges System — A-Z" | 22,000 |

**This is Rauf's home turf and he is good at it.** It is also 57.6% of everything he makes.

### BOF — decision stage, proof that a person like me got a result

Not "here's how it works" — **"here is it working, on camera, repeatedly, including when
it didn't."** Job: make buying feel safe.

| Channel | Example | Views |
|---|---|---|
| JJ | "Every Trade And Payout From This Week ($50,000+ \| Ep. 9)" | 18,000 |
| JJ | "I Filmed Every Single Trade I Made This Week ($37,500 \| Ep. 8)" | 17,000 |
| JJ | "I Lost $30,000 in Prop Firm Profits in One Day (Ep. 3)" | 12,000 |
| JJ | "I Flew 4 Students to Miami to Trade Live With Me \| Ep. 7" | 12,000 |
| Hanck | "How We Scaled This Trader From $8k/m To $1,2M+/month" | 3,000 |
| Hanck | "how he went from $0 to $50k+ in 30 days (client interview)" | 899 |
| **Rauf** | **"He Went From Unprofitable to Hitting $14k+ in a SINGLE Day"** | **1,000** |

The four BOF sub-types both reference channels run:

1. **Serialized payout recap** — a running total that climbs weekly (JJ: Ep. 1–9)
2. **Loss video** — the one that makes the wins credible
3. **Student result** — interview, screen-share, or documented payout
4. **Behind-the-scenes** — the work being done, unpolished

Rauf has **one video across all four types**, and it is his lowest-viewed video ever.

## 3. The trap Rauf almost certainly fell into

Here is the single most important thing in this document.

**BOF content gets the worst view counts on every channel — including the ones winning.**

| Channel | TOF avg | MOF avg | **BOF avg** |
|---|---|---|---|
| JJ Simon | 29,326 | 37,100 | **13,416** |
| Hanck | 1,601 | 1,802 | **1,450** |
| Rauf | 6,433 | 8,738 | **1,000** *(n=1)* |

JJ's BOF averages **13,416 against a 19,000 channel median.** It is his worst-performing
category by a wide margin. **He allocates a third of his output to it anyway — and in the
last 30 days he pushed it to 41%.**

Rauf published one student-proof video, it did 1,000 views — his all-time worst — and he
never made another. That is a completely rational response to the view count and a
completely wrong response to the business.

> **BOF is not a reach format. It is the conversion layer.** Judging it by views is like
> judging a sales page by its bounce rate. The 12,000 people who watch a payout recap are
> a fundamentally different audience from the 119,000 who watch an aspiration video — they
> already know who you are, they have watched before, and they are the only ones who book.

Both winning channels accept a ~30% view penalty on a third of their content on purpose.
That is the trade Rauf has not made.

### 6.2 JJ's description ladder — verified

**Four rungs descending by commitment**, and note what each one is:

| Rung | What it is | Why it's there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The offer**, line one | Benefit-framed — *"Get Consistent Payouts Guaranteed"*, not "book a call" |
| 2 | **Free website / lead magnet** | Captures people who won't book yet but will take a free asset |
| 3 | **A student testimonial video** | Bottom-of-funnel proof for the unconvinced — content pointing at content |
| 4 | **Free Discord** | The lowest-commitment rung, last |

which video produced which click.

> **This is the same ladder Hanck runs** — offer first, then a free asset, then a proof
> video, then community. Two independent channels, same structure. Rung 3 is the one people
> miss: *the unready viewer gets routed into BOF proof content rather than lost.* That is a
> large part of why bottom-of-funnel content earns its slot despite the worst view counts —
> **it is the destination the other videos point at.**

### 6.5 Recommended in-video CTA pattern

*This is a recommendation derived from the landing-page structure and the description
ladder above, not a measurement of JJ's videos.*

Two CTAs per video, both short:

| When | Type | What it sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| **~2:00**, after the first proof beat | Soft, one line | *"Full system and my payout receipts are in the free training linked below."* Then keep teaching — do not dwell |
| **~80–85% through**, after the value is delivered | Hard, qualified | See below |
| End screen, last 20s | Visual | Call page + next video |

The hard CTA should **qualify and disqualify**, matching how JJ's page filters applicants:

> *"If you're trading a funded account or close to passing an evaluation, and you want me to
> build this around your schedule and your firm — there's an application linked below. It's
> not for beginners, and I take a limited number of students a month."*

Four jobs in one paragraph: qualifies (funded or close), sets scarcity (limited), **dis**qualifies
(not for beginners — which raises perceived value), and names the next step.

**Do not stack more than two.** The reference channels sell through *frequency of publishing*,
not frequency of asking — 10 videos a month each carrying two clean CTAs beats 5 videos
carrying five pushy ones.

| Channel | Median duration | Range |

| JJ Simon | 17m 19s | 7m – 50m |
| Rauf | 14m 09s | 9m – 61m |
| Hanck | 20m 44s | 8m – 301m |

### 7.3 The serialized show is the format Rauf is missing entirely

JJ runs **"Road to $1M / $2.5M In Prop Firm Payouts"** — nine numbered episodes, weekly,
each with the running total in the title:

> Ep. 1 ($87,000) → Ep. 2 ($105,700) → Ep. 3 (*lost $30,000*) → Ep. 4 → Ep. 5 → Ep. 6
> ($12,500, "why I'm not satisfied") → Ep. 7 (*flew students to Miami*) → Ep. 8 ($37,500)
> → Ep. 9 ($50,000+)

Three things this does that no standalone video can:

1. **A reason to come back on a schedule.** Episode numbers create appointment viewing.
2. **A number that visibly climbs.** The proof compounds across videos rather than resetting.
3. **Room for losses.** Ep. 3 is a $30,000 loss and Ep. 6 is a disappointing week. Inside a
   series those read as honesty; as standalone videos they would read as failure.

Rauf's only serialized content is **"Module 1 / 2 / 3 / 4"** — a curriculum, averaging
2,800–4,500 views. Curriculum numbering signals homework. Episode numbering signals a story.


## 8. What to change

### 8.1 The mix

Move to **8–10 videos/month** at this split:

| Stage | Target share | At 8/month | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOF | 40% | 3 | Prop-firm rankings, income aspiration, belief-shift essays |
| MOF | 28% | 2–3 | Time Based Ranges mechanism reveals — *cut from 4–5 to 2–3* |
| **BOF** | **32%** | **3** | **Payout recaps, losses, student results — currently 0** |

The change is not "make more videos." It is **stop converting BOF ideas into MOF videos.**
Rauf already films his own trading; he publishes it as *"The Boring 1-Hour Window That Made
Me $190,000"* (a mechanism reveal) instead of *"Week 4: $8,400 in payouts, two losses"*
(a payout recap). Same footage, different job.

### 8.2 The three BOF formats to start immediately

1. **Weekly serialized payout recap.** A running total that climbs. JJ's runs at ~14,000
   views — below his median, and it is the format that carries his sales. Number in the
   title, escalating every week.
2. **A loss video.** JJ's "I Lost $30,000 in One Day" did 12,000 views and is worth more
   than a 40,000-view strategy reveal, because it is the only content that makes the
   payout recaps believable.
3. **Student results.** He has one. He needs one a month, minimum. Name, figure, named
   prop firm.

### 8.3 Title rules, from his own data

**Do:**
- Put **prop firm / Apex / Lucid / funded / payout** in the title — his +55% keyword
- Lead with a **documented number**, not curiosity — his proof titles beat curiosity by 39%
- Keep the **named system** (Time Based Ranges) — his top 15 brand it 4x more than his bottom 15
- Keep the **simplicity words** — "Boring" (45,000), "Stupid Simple" (18,000), "All You Need" (14,000)

**Stop:**
- **"Nobody talks about this"** — 7 titles, and it is his weakest framing class
- **"Will shock you" / "Will change your 2026"** — 2,300 and 1,400 views
- **"Module 1/2/3/4"** — 2,800–4,500 views. Curriculum labels don't get clicked
- **Negation openers** — "I Stopped Using Chart Patterns" (1,600)
