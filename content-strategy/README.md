# Rauf YouTube → High-Ticket Content Strategy

A content and funnel plan for converting **@DayTradingRauf**'s YouTube channel into a
pipeline for a high-ticket trading mentorship — modelled on **@itsjjsimon**'s channel
and the operator model run by **HanckFX Business / Create And Collect**.

## The short version

Rauf and JJ Simon have **the same subscriber count** (25.1K vs 25.3K). JJ gets **4x the
views per video** on a channel less than half the age — and, more importantly, JJ's
channel points at a **booking page** while Rauf's points at a **free Discord**.

Rauf already has a working paid-ads → webinar → application → call → close funnel
(this repo is its dashboard). His 452,500 lifetime YouTube views are not connected to
it. That is the highest-leverage fix available and it is plumbing, not content.

## Order of operations

1. **Funnel first** (`02`) — a better video pointing at nothing is still pointing at nothing
2. **Retrofit the back catalog** (`04`) — 59 videos, 452K views, currently routed to a free room
3. **Then content** (`03`, `04`)
4. **Then optimize on measured revenue per video** (`06`)

## Sourcing and caveats

- Channel data pulled **2026-08-26** — complete catalogs (37 / 59 / 57 videos), not just first pages.
- **YouTube blocked transcript retrieval** from this environment on every available
  route. The operator's model is reconstructed from his complete 57-video catalog and
  his two live sales pages, which state the mechanics explicitly — not from the video's audio.
- The operator's headline figures ($1.2M/month) are **their own marketing claims** and
  are unverified.
- **Income claims and guarantees carry real legal exposure in trading education.**
  Only publish documented, receipted numbers. See `02-offer-and-funnel.md` §1.3.
