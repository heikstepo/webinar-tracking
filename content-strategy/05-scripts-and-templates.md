## 1. VSL script (8–14 minutes)

The video on the booking page. Runs on the same logic as JJ's page.

**1. Hook (0:00–0:30)**
> "I've taken $XXX,XXX in verified prop firm payouts trading one 30-minute window a
> day. Not scalping all session, not sitting at the screen for eight hours — one window.
> In the next ten minutes I'll show you the receipts, show you exactly how the system
> works, and tell you how to get me to build it around your schedule and your firm."

**2. Proof (0:30–2:00)**
Screen-share the payout receipts. Name the firms. Show the dates. Say the cumulative
number out loud. *Show, don't claim.*

**3. Origin (2:00–4:00)**
The struggle → the discovery. Rauf already has this video (*"I Struggled for Years
Until I Learned This About Trading"*). The turn: he stopped trying to predict
*direction* and started measuring *time*.

**4. The mechanism (4:00–8:00)**
Explain Time Based Ranges properly. Live chart. Real examples. **Give away the how.**
The prospect needs to believe the system is real before they'll pay for help
implementing it.

**5. Why most people still fail with it (8:00–10:00)**
The bridge to the offer. They don't know which window fits their schedule; they can't
tell a valid range from an invalid one without feedback; they break the rules under
pressure with no accountability. *Every one of these is solved by a person, not a PDF —
which is exactly what's being sold.*

**6. The offer (10:00–12:00)**
What it is, who it's for, who it isn't for, capacity, the guarantee. **No price.**

**7. Close (12:00–13:00)**
> "There's an application below. It takes two minutes. If you're funded or close to
> passing an evaluation, fill it out and we'll get on a call and map out exactly which
> window fits your schedule. If you're brand new to futures, this isn't for you yet —
> go watch the free content first."

## 4. Typeform application

Qualification is the point. A shorter form books more calls; a *better* form books
better calls. Optimize for close rate, not booking volume.

1. Name / Email / Phone
2. What's your current situation? — *Never traded futures · Trading a demo · In an evaluation · Funded, not consistent · Funded and profitable*
3. Which prop firms have you used? *(open)*
4. Have you taken a payout before? — *No · Yes, under $5k · Yes, $5k–$25k · Yes, $25k+*
5. How much time can you sit at the charts each day? — *<30 min · 30–60 min · 1–2 hrs · 2+ hrs*
6. What's the single biggest thing stopping you right now? *(open — **use this verbatim on the call**)*
7. This is a paid mentorship starting in the $4,000 range. Are you in a position to invest if it's a fit? — *Yes · No · Need to understand it first*
8. Anything else I should know? *(open)*

**Q7 is the most important question on the form.** It removes the price shock from the
call and lets closers spend the whole call on fit instead of on affordability.

## 5. Closer call frame (45–60 min)

| Phase | Min | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Rapport + frame | 0–5 | "I'll ask questions, then tell you if this is a fit. If it isn't, I'll say so." |
| Discovery | 5–25 | Current state, what they've tried, what it's cost them, what funded would change |
| Gap | 25–35 | Reflect the distance between where they are and where they said they want to be |
| Present | 35–45 | Only the parts that solve *their* stated gap. Not a feature tour |
| Price + close | 45–55 | State it plainly. Silence. Handle objections |
| Onboard or disqualify | 55–60 | Payment and next steps, or an honest "not yet" |

**Objections, pre-handled by the content:**

| Objection | The content that already answered it |
|---|---|
| "Does this actually work?" | Payout recaps — including the losses |
| "Works for you, not me" | Student proof videos |
| "I don't have time" | The 30-minute-window positioning, in every title |
| "Too expensive" | Q7 on the application |
| "I'll do it myself" | True — the mechanism is free. What's sold is feedback and accountability |

This is the real argument for giving the mechanism away: **content that pre-handles
objections raises close rate**, and close rate moves revenue harder than view count.

## 6. Testimonial capture

Ask every student who takes a payout, the day they take it:

1. Where were you before you started? *(specific — losses, blown evals, time wasted)*
2. What changed? *(the mechanism)*
3. What are the numbers? *(payout figure + named prop firm)*
4. Who would you tell to do this?

Then: **written permission to publish name, figure, and firm.**

Format for the landing page — match JJ's, because specificity is what makes it work:

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ *"After being with Rauf a few months I passed Lucid and started taking
> payouts. $40k+ so far."* — **@handle**, Lucid

Generic praise converts nobody. A name, a number, and a firm converts.
