So we've got our 2 boxes.


They tell us where the orders are, and when the market's going to go for them.


What they don't tell us yet is which way we're trading.


And this is the part everyone assumes is the hard part.


Because you think you have to predict it.


Check the news, look at a few higher timeframes, form an opinion… and hope you're right.


That's what most inconsistent traders are doing at 9:30 in the morning.


And here's what that actually looks like on a normal day.


Market spikes. You buy. It reverses. Stops you out.


You're annoyed, so you flip and sell. And it reverses again.


Anyone done that? Put it in the chat.


Right. All of us.


And that's not a discipline problem.


You're making the biggest decision of your entire day in the most violent 30 seconds of the session,


with nothing in front of you to base it on.


Nobody makes a good decision in that state. Nobody.


So here's the good news — and this is the part that took the pressure off for me completely.


You don't have to predict anything.


Because before this market can make a real move in either direction, it has to do one thing first.


It has to go and collect the orders sat on one side.


That's not an opinion. That's just how it works.


And you already know exactly where those orders are, because you marked them in step 1.


So you're not forecasting anymore. You're watching.


The market is about to walk into one of your boxes.


All you have to do is see which side it goes for.


That's your direction, and it gets handed to you.


So by the end of this section you'll sit down at 9:29 tomorrow, watch one thing happen,


and know whether you're a buyer or a seller today. With an actual reason behind it.


Now — 2 things have to happen. And they happen in this order.
