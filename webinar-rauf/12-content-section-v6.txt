RAUF WEBINAR — CONTENT SECTION (v6)

Built on Fladlien's content architecture:
  - 4 steps + an unnumbered bridge
  - every step runs CONTEXT → VISION → STRATEGY → COMMITMENT
  - Step 1 = steps within the step. Step 2 = criteria. Step 3 = criteria. Step 4 = steps.
  - one anchor day threaded through all four steps (continuity)
  - every commitment is a choice, never a yes/no
  - every transition phrased as if the step is already done

Teaching the WHAT, not the HOW.
