3. THE OPPORTUNITY - WHY TIME
================================================================================

So let me tell you why I built everything around time, because otherwise the
windows I just showed you are going to sound arbitrary.

Everyone knows the statistic. Ninety five percent of traders lose, five percent
win. Everybody repeats it. Nobody does anything with it.

So I asked the obvious question. Who's the five percent?

And then I looked at what everybody is actually being taught. Every course, every
channel, every Discord. And it's all the same. Same patterns, same indicators, same
lens.

So we're all in the ninety five. All of us. Doing the same thing and confused about
getting the same result.

Which means getting better at what everyone else does can't be the answer. You have
to look at the market through a lens most people aren't using.

So I asked - what on a chart actually repeats? Not usually works. What repeats,
every single day, no exceptions?

It's not patterns. A head and shoulders looks identical whether price is about to
break or about to reverse. Same shape, opposite outcomes. You cannot build a
business on that.

It's not indicators either. An indicator is a maths problem run on price that has
already happened. It's history.

But look at your chart. Price up the side. Time along the bottom.

You have no idea what candle is about to print. None. But you know exactly when
it's going to print. To the second. Every day. Forever.

Time is the only repeatable variable on a chart.

And once you accept that, the question changes completely. It stops being what
should I trade, and it becomes when does this market actually do something.

That's what a time based range is. It's not an indicator, it's not a pattern. It's a
window where the same thing happens, over and over, and you can go and check it
yourself against a hundred and twenty eight days of data.

That's the opportunity. And I'd argue it's the only genuine edge left, because
everyone else is still arguing about which moving average to use.
