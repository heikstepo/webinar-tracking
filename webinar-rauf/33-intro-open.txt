WHAT CHANGED AND WHY

1. Cut "I'm not showing you this to impress you" and "you're going to think I'm lying to you."
   Both of those hand the audience an objection before they've had it. Nobody watching minute
   one has decided you're a liar yet. You're answering a question that hasn't been asked, and
   it reads defensive.

2. The bridge out of the dashboard is now the thing that's actually interesting about it:
   it came from the same trade at the same time of day. That's a claim the three showings
   directly prove, so the proof lands right where the claim was made.

3. "It all came from me trading my time-based ranges strategy" moved to AFTER the three trades.
   Before them it's a brand name with nothing behind it. After them it's a label for something
   they just watched happen three times.

4. The "30 minutes a day" claim moved down for the same reason. Before the trades it's a big
   claim on faith. After the trades it's a description of what they just saw — three setups
   with no analysis in any of them.

5. Cut "In a second I'll tell you the story of how I even came up with it and exactly how AND
   WHY it works, step by step" and "But just to show you a glimpse of it, check this out."
   Both were stalling. The loop is now one line and it sits after the proof, not before it.

6. Cut "Every trading day looks the exact same for me." It over-claims — you say later that
   the setup shows 1 to 3 times a week, which contradicts "every day."

OPEN ITEMS

- DATE to DATE on the five-day window.
- "About 30 minutes of work a day" — confirm that's the number you want to stand behind on camera.
