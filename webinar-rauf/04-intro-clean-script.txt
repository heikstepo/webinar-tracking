WHY TIME - SELLING THE MECHANISM
================================================================================

So let me tell you why I ended up building everything around time, because if I
just start showing you windows and boxes it's going to sound arbitrary.

Here's the question I asked after that fifteen thousand dollar day.

What on a chart actually repeats?

Not usually works. Not works in trending markets. What repeats, every single day,
without exception, whether I like it or not?

And it's not patterns. A head and shoulders looks exactly the same whether price
is about to break or about to reverse. Same shape, two opposite outcomes. You
can't build a business on that.

It's not indicators either, because an indicator is just a maths problem run on
the price that already happened. It's telling you about the past.

But look at your chart. Price is up the side. And time is along the bottom.

You have no idea what candle is about to form. None. But you know exactly when
it's going to form. To the second. Every day. Forever.

Time is the only repeatable variable on a chart.

And I've said this before in a completely different context, but it's true here
too - time doesn't respond to big intentions. Time responds to things that get
repeated.

So if time is the only thing that repeats, and everybody's building their strategy
on the thing that doesn't - that's the gap. That's the five percent.

And when I started looking at the market that way, I found something I genuinely
couldn't unsee. There are specific windows in the day where the market does the
same thing. Not similar. The same thing.

And I can prove it with data that isn't mine - which I'll show you shortly, and
which I think is going to be the part of today you screenshot.

[PLACEHOLDER - Rauf: is there anything you can say here about WHY those specific
