=====================================================
TWO THINGS I DELIBERATELY DID NOT DO
=====================================================

  1. Never called it a win rate.
     He does, in that video — "it's going to show you even higher win rate."
     But the number measures how often the market double breaks, not how often
     a trade wins. Stating a win rate is a performance claim with nothing behind
     it, and the gap is big enough for someone to pull apart on the call.
     "How often the double break happened" is accurate and just as strong.

  2. Left out "anything above 50% you can make money with."
     True-ish, but it's an invitation to argue about R:R and expectancy, and it
     drags the section into maths. The 0% line is the better closer.


=====================================================
WHY THE BAD DAYS ARE IN
=====================================================

  Wednesday at 61% is the most persuasive number on the page.
  Saying "here's the day my own system is weakest, and here's when I skip it"
  is the single cheapest credibility available in the whole webinar — it costs
  nothing and it's the one thing his competitors structurally can't copy,
  because they don't have day-level data to be honest about.
