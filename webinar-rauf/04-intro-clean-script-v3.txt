Built on Heik's intro framework, applied to Rauf.
Clean read script. The only production note is: show live
Anything in [SQUARE BRACKETS] is a gap for Rauf to fill.

Runs approx 24 minutes, plus pre-roll.

HEIK'S ORDER, WHICH THIS FOLLOWS
   1. Opening hook - live proof, then transition to why they're here
   2. Position as the authority - immediately
   3. Sell them on the unique mechanism
   4. Create commitments
   5. Open loops
   Objections are not a section - they are handled throughout, mostly
   through testimonials.

WHERE WE DEVIATE, AND WHY
   - The hook shows live proof AND names the mechanism in the first two
     minutes. Heik's example only shows a revenue dashboard. Rauf's edge is
     that the mechanism itself is visible on a chart, so we show both. The
     full why still waits for section 3.
   - Heik uses five slides of open loops. Five loops, but tightened into one
     block so it doesn't drag.

3. SELL THEM ON THE MECHANISM
================================================================================

So let me tell you why I ended up building everything around time, because
otherwise those two lines I drew are going to sound arbitrary.

Everyone knows the statistic. Ninety five percent of traders lose, five percent
win. Everybody repeats it and nobody does anything with it.

So after that fifteen thousand dollar day, I asked the obvious question. Who's the
five percent?

And then I looked at what everybody is actually taught. Every course. Every
channel. Every Discord. It's all the same - same patterns, same indicators, same
lens on the chart.

So we're all the ninety five. All of us. Doing the same thing and confused about
getting the same result.

Which means getting better at what everyone else is doing cannot be the answer. You
have to look at the market through a lens most people aren't using.

So I asked - what on a chart actually repeats? Not usually works. Not works in
trending markets. What repeats, every single day, no exceptions?

It's not patterns. A head and shoulders looks identical whether price is about to
break or about to reverse. Same shape, opposite outcomes. You cannot build a
business on that.

It's not indicators. An indicator is a maths problem run on price that's already
happened. It's history with a nice colour on it.

But look at your chart. Price is up the side. Time is along the bottom.

You have no idea what candle is about to print. None. But you know exactly when
it's going to print. To the second. Every day. Forever.

Time is the only repeatable variable on a chart.

And the moment you accept that, the question changes. It stops being what should I
trade, and it becomes when does this market actually do something.

Now here's the part that made me stop arguing with it.

But that's not the number I care about. This is the number.

Zero.

Zero days out of a hundred and twenty eight where that range didn't break at all.
Not one. Every single day, something happened at that level.

Think about what that actually means for how you trade. You are never again sitting
at the screen hoping a setup shows up. The setup is coming. It's scheduled. Your
only job is to be there and know what to do when it arrives.

And you can go and verify every bit of that yourself. I'm not asking you to believe
me.

That's the opportunity. And honestly I think it's the last real edge available to a
