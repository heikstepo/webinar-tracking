WHY THESE 5

They're the 5 objections most likely to still be alive after the pitch:
  money, time, "I've been burned before", capital, and "1on1 is a lie".

Everything else we've killed earlier. Belief in the strategy died in the content section.
"Will it keep working" died with the third party data. "What if I fail" died with the
guarantee.

Question 1 is money and it goes first on purpose. Fladlien's logic: most people say no the
first time, so get the no out of the system early and every answer after it is a fresh
chance at yes. Same reason the AI App deck opens on it.

Question 5 goes last because it's the answer that best justifies the 10-spot limit, which
is what you want ringing in their ears going into open Q&A.

THE CLOSE AFTER EVERY ANSWER

Q1, Q2 end on a direct instruction to go and fill out the form. Q3, Q4, Q5 end on the
argument itself, because closing on all 5 starts to sound desperate. Then the holding
lines carry the asks through open Q&A, which is where Heik says the majority of
applications actually come in. Stay on 30-45 minutes minimum.
