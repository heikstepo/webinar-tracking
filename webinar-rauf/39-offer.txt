WHAT I CHANGED FROM YOUR OPENING

You had "It's a 6-month program, structured in 2 phases: Phase 1... Phase 2..."

That's now "It's a 12-week intensive bootcamp, and there are 4 parts to it." It has to be,
because Phase 2 is Bonus 1 — if he announces it in the core breakdown, it can't land as a
surprise 10 minutes later. The payoff line "that's 6 months, not 12 weeks" only works if
6 months hasn't already been promised.

Everything else of yours is intact: the "NOT a course" beat, "hands-on consulting", the
guarantee tease, "so how does it work".

STRUCTURE, AGAINST THE AI APP DECK

Same spine: introducing -> what it isn't -> how it works -> 4 core parts each with a STACK
slide -> "most students had only this and STILL got these results" -> "but since you're
here LIVE" -> 4 bonuses each solving a named problem, STACK after each -> "saved this one
for last" -> guarantee -> scarcity (first 10, bonuses come off, core stays) -> application
-> pre-empted questions into Q&A.

The one thing Rauf has that the AI App offer doesn't is a real guarantee, so it gets its
own beat before scarcity. It's the strongest single asset in this pitch and it should not
be buried inside the bonus stack.

HOW THE BONUSES MAP TO THE TRANSITION

Every bonus answers something the audience already agreed would happen to them:

  go-alone problem                     bonus that kills it
  "3 losers and you quit"           -> Bonus 1 (you don't stop at week 12)
  "failed a challenge, paid again"  -> Bonus 2 (Rauf pays, and only when ready)
  "you got away with it"            -> Bonus 3 (send it to me, I'll tell you what happened)
  all of it                         -> Bonus 4 (every 2 weeks, on your charts)

Bonus 3 absorbed the trade-review function you cut, which is why it's written around
"you can't tell if you followed the model" rather than around convenience.
