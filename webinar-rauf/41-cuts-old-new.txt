A NOTE ON WHERE THE TIME ACTUALLY IS

Animated builds are cheap. A 6-beat build costs about 25 seconds total.
LIVE chart trips are expensive — 90 seconds minimum, usually 2 minutes.

So the big savings below are all about removing chart trips and long runs of
separate text slides. I've left most animated builds alone.

=========================
STEP 5 — FULL TRADES
=========================

DON'T CUT THIS. RE-SHAPE IT.

Right now it's 6 examples at roughly equal weight, ~13 minutes.

The argument Step 5 is making is "it repeats." Equal weight kills that argument,
because by example 3 the audience is sitting through the same length of
explanation again and it just feels slow.

NEW SHAPE, same total time:

  Trade 1   3-4 min    All four steps, narrated in full, slowly.
  Trade 2   90 sec     "Range. There's the break. There's the entry. Out."
  Trade 3   45 sec
  Trade 4   30 sec
  Trade 5   30 sec
  Trade 6   20 sec     "Range, break, entry, out. Next."

The acceleration IS the argument. By trade 5 they're finishing the sentences in
their head before Rauf says them, and that feeling is worth more than any
individual example.
