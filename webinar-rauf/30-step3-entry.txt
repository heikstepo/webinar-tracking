1. THE 3 CANDLE HOURLY SWING POINT — the whole teaching block. ~180 words + a live block.

   It was a 4th concept in a step that already has 3, and it needed the most
   explaining for the least payoff. It's also the only thing in step 3 that isn't
   an entry — it's a confirmation — so it forced a "this is not where you buy"
   caveat that added confusion.

   I kept the idea as one line: "I want the hourly candle to agree with me as well,
   because a 5 minute chart can lie to you and the hour won't." Plus his own
   "treat your hourly candles like your daily candles" line, which is too good to lose.

   ⚠️ If you'd rather keep it in full, say so and I'll put it back — but it's the
   single biggest length win available in this step.


2. "Down close candle. Then another one lower. Then another one lower."
   Compressed to one sentence. Same setup, 40 words less.


3. "And anything that forms in a liquidity pool, or at one, is very high probability."
   Cut. It's vague, and it arrives after the OB explanation has already made the point.


4. "The one the algorithm used to do the job."
   Cut. "That's the candle that took everyone's stops" already says it, and
   "the algorithm" invites a question we don't want to open here.
