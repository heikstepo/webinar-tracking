THE 3 "KNOWING WHAT YOU KNOW NOW" BULLETS

Number 3 is the important one. It closes the loop the intro opened — "psychology
problems are really strategy problems, because a good strategy takes the big decisions
away from you." Asking them to agree with it here, out loud, right before the two
choices, means the go-alone problems below can't be read as "you'll lack discipline."
They read as "you'll execute it wrong", which is what the offer fixes.

THE 3 GO-ALONE PROBLEMS

All three are execution failures, not character failures. Deliberate, same reason.
Each maps onto a piece of the offer:
 - 2 of those weren't my setup      -> personal trade reviews, live sessions 3x a week
 - you got away with it             -> personal trade reviews, bi-weekly 1on1
 - failed on a rule you didn't know -> prop firm playbooks + Rauf pays for the challenge

THE 3 OPTION-TWO PROMISES

Deliberately ordered so the last one is the outcome, not a feature. "Get funded, and if
you're not funded I keep working with you until you are" is the guarantee in one line —
it lands here as a promise, then gets its full treatment in the pitch. That's the setup
Fladlien calls persuasional foreplay: create the demand here, satisfy it there.
