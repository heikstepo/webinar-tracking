Sources:

- **Discovery call: Shikha Miglani (30 Sep 2026).** Same new positioning.

**Why this batch matters most so far:** Mario and Shikha are the first calls that fit the Meta ICP. Both are small, founder-led companies (Mario's is about $1.2M), and both heard the 30-in-30 offer live.

## 1. Mario Silva (Miami): closest ICP match so far

### Profile
- Founder, about 4 years in. The firm runs veteran benefits claims on a contingency model.
- 150-210 claims a year at $8-16k each, so about $1.2M revenue. Wants 400 claims a year.
- Growth path: 20 → 60 → 100 → 210 claims.
- No sales team. Has a VP Ops, a support team and advisors.
- **About 90% of business is referral.**
- Sold B2C (veterans), so technically off-ICP. Alex pitched it anyway ("B2B2C works on LinkedIn").
- Came in by replying to outbound email.

### Verbatims
- **Referral fear:** "about a 90% referral rate... one day might be six months might be three years might be five years from now that referral pipeline might dry up"
- "I would like to get there faster. And again, I would like to be prepared for when the pipeline of referrals ends or dries out."
- **Ran Meta ads himself:** "about a six-month campaign on Meta Ads, got about 2,000 leads. But the conversion for those leads... less than 3%"
- Also spoke at a seminar of about 2,000 people and converted 20-25.
- Alex reframed it: "the main issue is conversion vs volume." Mario: "Yes. Exactly."
- **The team expects 20-30% conversion; reality is 2-8%.** "even 8% it's hard."
- **Reaction to 30-in-30 (validates the hook live):** "I like what you said about, hey, you'll give me 30 qualified ICP leads within the first 30 days. If not, then there's a refund guarantee. So I was like, okay, well, that's a good offer."
- "I would like to see it in action and see how it works before... that's why that 30-day guarantee kind of caught my interest"
- **Relief that it isn't video:** "So it's not like making video interviews with me... because that's a lot of time."
- **Budget pain at the $1M level:** about $60k a year is "about 5% of our revenue... pretty much the full marketing budget for the year"
- **Leads are not clients:** "Out of the 30 leads, we convert one or two or something or even zero... then that's when we have to determine the ROI doesn't fit"
- He needed an ROI mock-up for his accounting team.

### Takeaways for ads
1. **Referrals close and ads don't.** The gap is trust. LinkedIn authority creates referral-level trust before the first conversation. The strongest unifying angle so far.
2. The ICP has probably **run Meta ads and got cheap, cold leads that didn't close.** "Warm vs cold" will land hard with them.
3. The bottom of the $1-10M band feels the price. At $1.2M, the PoV is "the full marketing budget." Funnel economics risk: see section 7.

## 2. Shikha Miglani (Philadelphia): AI founder

### Profile
- Founder of an AI product and consulting company (LLM scientists on the team).
- Deals of $200-250k, up to $2M. Wants $1-2M "right now."
- Expanding to Dubai and the US. Shopping around, including a competitor called **Windmill.** Wants to start in November.

### Verbatims
- **Sales over marketing:** "marketing is nice. Everybody looks at you, wow... but the real deal is closing the deal and getting the money in the bank"
- "every dollar that goes has to generate a value"
- "can I trust you guys to bring me sales"
- **Shame about self-promotion:** "if you're a commoner you are like oh my god I'm showing off every day, people must be sick of you, you have this shame around it that holds you back"
- **Authentic over loud:** "I don't want to be all sold out loud... even if I'm like 80% of where I want to be but still the right path I would prefer that than going 120%... just all over with my branding"
- "there's a hesitation of writing every day yourself"
- **The AI content backlash:** "when everybody's writing from GPT it becomes very boring, so now we have to make content human"
- **Trust from visible traction:** "That one time, 198 likes. Believe me, it builds trust like anything because people buy based on what they see and how they feel about it."
- **Products that nobody sees:** "I already have products out there. What's the point of them being out there? I'm not selling them."
- **Founder-led sales before a sales team:** her "AI consultant" said "before breaking into a big half a million dollar sales team, go with the founder route of selling, get proof of concepts"
- **Wanted an even shorter trial:** "I was hoping it's one month to see"
- **Market price anchors:**
  - Freelancers at about $3k/month ("lone warriors").
  - Influx at $4.9k is "within the budget... as long as it can generate sales."
  - "If it's 5,000 for nothing... it might pinch me."

### Live AEO proof (big)
- She researched Influx **in ChatGPT.** It compared Influx with Windmill and told her Influx costs "about $15,000 per month," which is wrong. She nearly didn't take the call because of it.
- This proves buyers use AI search to shortlist vendors. It also shows what happens when you don't control your narrative: AI fills the gap, sometimes wrongly.
- Ad angle: "Your next client will ask ChatGPT about you. What will it say?"

## 7. Strategic observations after batch 3
1. **Referral dependence is the dominant pain across the pipeline.** Mario: "90% referral." OJ: reference selling. Martyn: word of mouth. Supersonic: cold calling. Everyone's best clients come through trust. The message is "make every lead feel like a referral."
2. **The ICP has probably been burned by cold lead gen,** including Meta ads (Mario: 2,000 leads, under 3% conversion). "Warm, not more" is a natural contrast. It's slightly ironic to say it in a Meta ad, but it works if it's framed as "leads who already know you."
3. **Shame and self-promotion is a real blocker** that hasn't been addressed in creative yet. Shikha: "I'm showing off every day, people must be sick of you." Gary: "I'm an old guy, not trying to become anything." Gary: "interesting maybe in the bakery world, but not more broadly."
4. **ChatGPT is already shaping buyer shortlists,** and it got Influx's price wrong. This is real evidence for the AI-search angle.
5. **Funnel economics risk.** The Typeform gate is about $500k, but at $1.2M Mario felt the PoV as "5% of revenue, the full marketing budget." Ads that pull the bottom of the band may book calls that can't afford the offer. Worth considering callouts that skew to "$1M+" or "$2-10M."
6. **The new offer works on live calls.** The guarantee was the moment Mario leaned in. Shikha asked about a one-month trial unprompted. Lead with the risk reversal.
