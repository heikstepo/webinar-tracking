## The 12 slots for Influx

```
1 · Audience signal
- "B2B company", "founder", "referrals", "your buyers"
- one per hook, in line 1

2 · Fake fix
- posting yourself when you find time
- a ghostwriter or freelancer
- AI tools
- someone on the team
- reposting the company page
- cold lead gen (Meta ads, cold email): leads that don't know you and don't close

3 · The judge
- their buyers: decision-makers who check them out on LinkedIn (and ChatGPT) before they buy

4 · Real criterion
- "Have I seen this person before… and do they know what they're talking about?"
- Alex: "the trusted brands are the ones that are visible and consistent"

5 · Catch-22
- "LinkedIn only works if I post every week… and I can't post every week because I'm running the company."

6 · How we do it (not named in ads, we sell done-for-you, not a method)
- we capture how you think in 1 hour, we write your posts in your voice, our network gets them in front of your buyers, you get a weekly list of who's warm
- each ad opens on their exact problem, then we come in and do it for them. Only explain the part of "how" that fixes that ad's problem

7 · Analogy (Alex's own, from the calls)
- a snowball down a hill / jump-starting a car: hard to get moving, compounds once it rolls
- the Media Engine is the jump start

8 · "Even if" fear
- you finally post… 12 likes, all from your own team
- a post goes out that sounds like AI, and in a small industry people notice

9 · Support after the win
- weekly list of potential clients who engaged, to start conversations with
- optional: a dedicated setter books the conversations (never a call number)

10 · Risk removal
- time: 1 hour once, then about 10 minutes a month
- voice: nothing goes live without your approval
- account: we never need your password, zero bans

11 · Wrong buyer
- freelancers, solo consultants, real estate agents, job seekers, enterprise marketing teams, people who want to learn to post themselves

12 · Money filter
- B2B, [$1M]-$10M revenue, 10-20 people, founder still closes the deals
```

Keep out of hooks: impressions, likes, followers. That's the commodity part and it pulls creators and coaches.
