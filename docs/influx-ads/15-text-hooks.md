# Influx on-screen text hooks (master)

9 Nov 2026. One text hook per ad in "Influx - Ad Scripts November 2026". Heik: "for each ad I need a text hook on screen that doesn't repeat the scripted hook but adds on top of it, to retain people and hook them in 2 ways. It still has to be tied to the actual ad." Length and logic from the Ecom MBA VSL ad scripts (v10), not their words.

How the Ecom MBA ones work: 4 to 11 words, and they do a different job from the spoken hook.
- Spoken hook is a story or a claim → the text calls out the viewer's situation ("scared to order inventory for your product idea?" over "In [YEAR], I over-ordered…").
- Spoken hook is a callout or the offer → the text adds the payoff, curiosity or credibility ("new way of building ecom brands (after $34M+ in sales)").
- Formats: a question about their situation, "X vs. Y", "→", "warning: before you…", "watch this first", a bracketed credential.

Same safety as the primary text: no money, no "guaranteed", no media engine.
