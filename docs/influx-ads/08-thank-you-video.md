# Influx pre-call thank-you video (master)

v1, 15 Oct 2026. Plays on the confirmation page right after someone books. Alex to camera. About 330 words, a little over 2 minutes.

Built on the PM Rev and Iron pre-call TY videos, plus the useful parts of Alex's old confirmation video (accept the invite, what to bring):
1. Greeting and congrats on booking.
2. Who they'll speak to and what the call is for (the offer in one line).
3. The goal until the call: clarity, so they already know if it makes sense.
4. Lock the call in (accept the invite).
5. The team may reach out with a few questions, so the call goes straight into their plan.
6. Short FAQ videos and case studies on the page, named.
7. An honest picture: the average client result (Iron's "not the best one, the average one").
8. Honest expectation, then "you're in really good hands".
9. The to-dos again, and bring the other decision makers (B2B version of PM's "bring your partner").
10. Sign-off.

Open before filming:
- [$X] average extra revenue in the first 90 days (same number as the VSL's pricing section).
- "You'll be speaking with me directly": Alex's own VSL says "with me directly or a member of my team". Match whatever the calendar actually books.
- The page needs: FAQ videos (sounds like me, media engine and account safety, what happens if we miss the 30 calls) and Scott and Ryan case study videos.
- The calendar invite goes out automatically on booking (Alex's confirmation video says it does).

```
Hey, Alex here, and congrats on booking your call.

You'll be speaking with me directly, to see how we can build your LinkedIn brand into an inbound acquisition system for your company…

And book you 30 qualified calls in the next 90 days.

Between now and our call, my goal is to give you all the clarity you need…

So by the time we talk, you already have a good idea if working with us makes sense for you.

First, make sure you accept the calendar invite we just sent you, so the call is locked in on your end.

Before the call, my team might also reach out with a few quick questions about your company, your ICP, and your LinkedIn…

That way, we can look at everything beforehand and get straight into your plan on the call.

On top of that, you'll find a few short videos on this page that answer the questions I get asked the most…

Like whether the posts will actually sound like you…

How our media engine works, and whether your account is safe…

What happens if we don't hit the 30 calls…

And more.

You'll also see how founders like Scott and Ryan used the Authority Engine to turn their LinkedIn into an inbound acquisition channel.

And just so you have an honest picture…

Our average client, not the best one, the average one, makes [$X] in extra revenue in their first 90 days with us.

I'm not going to tell you the calls start coming in on day one…

The first few weeks are about launching and testing what works for your ICP…

But with a team that's done this over and over for B2B founders, you're in really good hands.

So, accept the invite, go through the videos below, and if my team reaches out, take a minute to answer their questions.

And if anyone else is part of decisions like this, like a co-founder or your head of sales, bring them onto the call as well.

Looking forward to speaking with you.
```
