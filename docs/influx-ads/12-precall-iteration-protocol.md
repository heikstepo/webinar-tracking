# Influx pre-call emails: iteration protocol

Started 26 Oct 2026. Heik reviews each email and sends back his redone version. After every round, the diff between my draft and his version goes into the log below, and anything that applies beyond that one email becomes a rule in the checklist. Read this file before writing the next email.

## Checklist before sending an email

Baseline (v0), from the analysis page, the reference sequences and the copy rules so far. Rules learned from Heik's rewrites get added under "Learned from Heik".

Format
- Plain text from Alex. "SL:" line, then "Hey {{first_name}}," and straight into the point. Signed "- Alex".
- Subject line in lower case except the name, personal and specific: their question, the promise, a hook, or proof.
- One short line per paragraph, the same line style as the ads and the VSL. "…" only at line ends.
- Most emails 150-300 words. The engager is one line; how it works can go to about 400.

Flow
- Flow first, then clarity, then length. Every line has to lead into the next one, and every turn needs a connector ("Now,", "So", "Because", "And").
- Answer first in objection emails, then why it comes up, the specifics, the proof, one honest line, and close on the call ("On our call, we'll…").

Words
- Easy words, spoken, ICP lingo: ICP, ICP leads, convert, booked calls, qualified calls, inbound acquisition system, decision makers, B2B founders, 7 and 8 figure.
- Say it the way the VSL, TY video and FAQs say it: the offer line, the Authority Engine, the media engine, one hour to onboard, about 10 minutes a month, "mid to high four figures a month", the guarantee line.
- Never: "buyers", "the right people", "here's the thing", "the catch is", "I'm not guessing", "missing", "turns into", "the math is pretty simple", warm-ups ("Fair question", "Honestly…", "I'll be honest"), stacked fragments ("No writing, no filming…"), "program", "course", "students", fake urgency.
- Proof in the proof format: "[X] new followers, [X] booked calls, [$X] in revenue". Pipeline is called pipeline, signed revenue is called revenue, exactly as Alex's videos say. First names only.

Fit
- Everything must match the ads and the VSL they came through. One off-message email is enough to cause a no-show.
- Never re-sell LinkedIn itself; they're already sold. Never talk to anyone smaller or newer.
- Placeholders in [brackets] for anything Alex hasn't confirmed.
