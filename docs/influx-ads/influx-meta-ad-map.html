Influx · Meta ads · Ad groups and angles

One campaign, eight jobs, twenty-four angles

The ad groups and angles for the 30-in-30 offer, built on your training's structure and grounded in the ICP and offer research. No scripts yet. Each angle is the idea a creative will carry, with the evidence behind it.

Prepared 9 Oct 2026Built on: Playbook · ICP Dossier · Offer Blueprint

The campaign

One ICP, so one campaign. The four ICP segments (Referral-Ceiling Founder, Invisible Expert, Founder-Seller, Burned Buyer) appear as angles inside the ads, not as separate campaigns.

Campaign
1 × CBO, leads objective
Ad set
1 broad control. US, 25 to 64, Advantage+ audience off.
Ads
8 jobs × 3 creatives, plus 2 primary texts and 2 headlines each
Conversion
Booked Discovery call
Budget
About $225 a day today

The belief chain

What a founder has to believe, in rough order, before booking a call. Each belief is one ad. Meta decides which ad each person sees next, so this is the set of messages, not a fixed sequence.

  1. "Relying on referrals is a risk, and buyers already judge me online."Ad 1
  2. "LinkedIn is now where buyers and AI decide who to trust."Ad 2
  3. "What I tried failed for reasons that can be fixed. LinkedIn does work for someone like me."Ad 3
  4. "There's a system that does this without eating my time or sounding fake."Ad 4
  5. "Trying it is low risk: 30 warm leads in 30 days, or I don't pay."Ads 5 and 8
  6. "It has worked for others, and the people running it practise it themselves."Ad 6
  7. "My last doubts have been answered."Ad 7

Callouts: the words are the targeting

Targeting is broad, so every creative's words decide who Meta finds. Ads about LinkedIn pull in job seekers, solo coaches and aspiring creators by default. The hook has to keep them out.

Put these in every hook

  • B2B
  • founder or CEO
  • $1M+ or $1M to $10M
  • 10 to 20 people
  • you still close the deals
  • referrals
  • clients
  • pipeline
  • buyers

Keep these out

  • personal brand
  • grow your following
  • go viral
  • influencer
  • content creator
  • job
  • career
  • LinkedIn tips
  • hacks
What a callout needs to do

State the company type, the revenue band, and that the founder still sells, all within the first sentence or two. For example: "If you run a B2B company doing over $1M and you're still the one closing the deals..." The 30-in-30 offer is no use to anyone that sentence filters out.

Guardrails for every creative

Check each script against this list before filming.

  • No em dashes
  • "Lead" defined on screen whenever the 30-in-30 is stated
  • Never claims Influx books calls or meetings
  • Case studies labelled "documented example", first names only, no company names
  • No pipeline dollars, impression guarantees or "100% organic"
  • Never describes how the Media Engine's page network works
  • Prospect stories fully anonymised, with no identifying details
  • Stats shown with a source on screen
  • Calm, peer-to-peer tone, no hype slogans
  • One job per creative, matching its ad

Launch and testing

Moving from the current ABO test to the training's structure.

StepWhatWhy
1Rebuild as one CBO campaign with one broad control ad set (US, 25 to 64, Advantage+ audience off)Gives sequence learning a clean team of ads to work with
2Load 8 ads with 3 creatives each, plus 2 primary texts and 2 headlinesCreative diversity without too many ads
3Optimise for booked Discovery calls. If there are too few for Meta to learn from, use qualified Typeform submits instead.Optimise for the deepest event that fires often enough
4Leave it alone for 7 to 14 daysDiscovery calls took one to two calls over a week to reach a decision
5Read each ad against its role. Ads 1 and 2 are top of funnel. Ads 3 and 4 are middle. Ads 5 to 8 are bottom.Find the weak link before replacing anything
6Check lead quality: Typeform pass rate, revenue band of bookings, show rateIf bookings come in under $1M, tighten the callouts, since the words are the targeting
7The first tests will probably be on Ads 2 and 3, with backups from 2.4, 3.4 and Ad 7These are the least proven messages
Small warm pool

Influx sold through outbound, so it has almost no warm audience on Meta. New ads won't get the easy warm-traffic start the training describes, and Ads 1 and 2 have to build the aware pool from day one. Expect the bottom-of-funnel ads to look weak in week one and improve as that pool grows.
