## Structure (confirmed by Heik, 26 Oct)

15 emails, sent in this order. If the call comes first, the rest don't go out, which is why the confirmation page email sits at 5: by then they've seen the gameplan, Alex's proof, how it works, and every case study and FAQ video. Objection emails are told through the client who had that objection, with their video linked. No day-before or day-of email (Heik cut everything after the John case study).

## Emails

### Email 1: Gameplan (v1, 26 Oct, Heik moved on to email 2 without changes)

Goes out right after booking. The VSL soft CTA's description of the call, the VSL CTA's "if it makes sense for both of us… no hard feelings", and the TY video's to-dos, so it all says the same thing. The reason we're selective is the guarantee, not Alex's time (the refs' "I physically can't take on more" doesn't fit a team-run DFY agency). [X] days to onboard is the VSL's placeholder.

```
SL: {{first_name}}, one thing before our call

Hey {{first_name}}, Alex here…

I saw you just booked a call with me, so I wanted to take a second and walk you through exactly what's going to happen on it…

Because I want you to get as much out of that call as possible.

First, we'll go through where your company's at right now…

How you're getting clients, who your ICP is, and what your LinkedIn looks like today…

And from there, we'll map out exactly how we'd build your LinkedIn into an inbound acquisition system for your company.

Now, since we guarantee every client 30 qualified calls in 90 days, we can only take on companies where we know we can hit that number…

So on the call, we'll also look at whether working together makes sense for both of us.

If it does, we'll get you onboarded within [X] days.

If it doesn't, I'll tell you that as well, no hard feelings…

And either way, you'll leave with a clear plan for your LinkedIn.

Before the call, there are just two things I need from you…

Accept the calendar invite we just sent you, or forward it to whoever runs your calendar, so the call is locked in…

And if anyone else is part of decisions like this, like a co-founder or your head of sales, bring them onto the call as well.

In the meantime, I'll answer every question you might have, so by the time we talk, you already know if this makes sense for you…

Some of them are already answered in the short videos on your confirmation page…

And I'll send you a few emails before our call with everything else.

So keep an eye on your inbox…

And I'll see you soon.

- Alex
```

### Email 2: Engager (v1, 26 Oct)

Same as both reference sequences, word for word in shape, because its whole job is a reply: a one-line question from Alex's own inbox gets answered, and once they've replied, the rest of the sequence lands in their main inbox, not promotions. "Our call" to match email 1. Goes out a few hours after email 1, or the next morning if the call is more than two days out.

```
SL: personal message for {{first_name}}

Hey {{first_name}}, any questions before our call?

- Alex

Sent from my iPhone
```

Needs: replies go to Alex's real inbox (or someone answering as Alex, same day).
