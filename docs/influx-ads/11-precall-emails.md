# Influx pre-call email sequence (master)

26 Oct 2026. From Alex, plain text, between booking and the discovery call. Analysis: influx-precall-emails.html.

## Proposed structure (Heik's 9 + additions)

Sent in this order. If the call is close, the later ones drop out, but the day-before and day-of emails always go.
