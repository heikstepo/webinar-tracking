## 1. Offer (Meta funnel)

- **Centerpiece:** 30 warm, qualified ICP leads within 30 days, or you do not pay.
- **"Lead" definition (required on screen):** warm, ICP-qualified commercial interest. A high-fit buyer who has engaged with your authority. NOT booked meetings.
- **Second half of the promise:** founder time protection. About 1 hour of onboarding, then approvals only.
- **Names for the mechanism:** LinkedIn Authority System, Authority Engine, Media Engine.
- **CTA:** Book a Discovery call (calendar runs evening slots in GST).
- **Selectivity:** limited installs at a time, and we will say no if it isn't a fit.

### Mechanism, as Alex explains it on calls
1. "Download your expertise" in a 1-hour onboarding.
2. Voice of the Customer doc, which needs approval (90% approved first time).
3. Sample content set, which also needs approval. These are the two gates before anything goes live.
4. The Media Engine: Influx owns pages across segments that engage early, which sends relevance signals so the algorithm matches the content to the right buyers. Alex's metaphors are "snowball down a hill" and "jump-start the car."
5. Live in about 7 days. Days 1-30 are a testing/launch phase, and day 31 onwards is scale.
6. Ongoing: 30 days of content sent at the start of each month, approved once per month. Weekly report every Tuesday, day by day.
7. Account access goes through a secure third-party encrypted platform, so Influx never sees passwords. Zero bans, compliant with LinkedIn's terms of service.
8. Formats: Impact Cards (visual statements) and long-form thought leadership. A newsletter add-on is available for AEO/GEO.
9. Scope options: one exec alone; exec + company page (recommended most often); or the wider leadership team. The minimum Alex recommends is 1 exec + company page, or 2 execs.

### Commercials (from calls, not for ads)
- 12-month standard retainer, or a 3-month proof of value.
- A performance-component option exists. Pricing is per "brand" (one profile or page).
- Two tiers: amplification only, or content + amplification.

## 3. ICP: TWO different audiences

**Meta VSL ICP (target):**
- B2B, about $1-10M revenue ($500k-$10M is acceptable framing).
- About 10-20 people, lean, founder-led.
- The founder is the bottleneck for content, leads and market perception.
- Explicitly NOT the "Axiom-scale" or 8-9-figure enterprise as the default callout.

**Outbound/sales-call prospects (batch 1) are mostly bigger:**
- Supersonic: 3,000 stores, bootstrapped, profitable, moving up to mid-market chains. Buyer is the founder or exec.
- Marigold: multi-brand SaaS. Buyer is the VP Marketing, with the CRO as sponsor.
- Rev1 Energy: electrical, commissioning, O&M and software for oil & gas and data centers. Buyer is the revenue lead; the CEO decides.
- StrikeReady: AI security startup, bridge round heading to Series B. Buyer is the CRO, who also owns marketing.

**Takeaway:** the calls are great for voice of customer and objections, but the persona sits a level above the Meta ICP. Reuse the pains and the language, and rescale the identity callouts to the founder of a 10-20 person company.

## 7. Market-shift arguments Alex uses
- LinkedIn went from "job search and talent platform" to "the modern buyer research layer."
- It is the #2 most cited domain in AI search, so it went from optional to mandatory.
- "Warm outbound": outbound converts better when the market already knows you.
- Brand building, but turned into "an actual engine that can drive revenue pipeline."
- Category claim: a hybrid of a lead gen agency and a PR agency.
