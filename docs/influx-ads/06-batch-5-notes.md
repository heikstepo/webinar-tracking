## 4. Semrush study (Adobe company), 89K LinkedIn URLs cited by ChatGPT Search, Google AI Mode and Perplexity
- LinkedIn is the #2 most cited domain (11.03%), just behind Reddit (11.29%), ahead of Wikipedia (9.53%) and YouTube (8.77%).
- ChatGPT Search cites LinkedIn in 13.5% of answers, Google AI Mode 14.3%, Perplexity 5.3%.
- About 95% of cited posts are original. Reshares are about 5%.
- 54-65% of cited posts share knowledge or advice. Company updates are only 7-9%.
- About 75% of cited authors post 5+ times in 4 weeks.
- Median cited post has 15-25 reactions. You don't need to go viral.
- People with under 500 followers are just as likely to be cited.
- "If your brand isn't consistently publishing on LinkedIn, someone else's content will fill that space and shape what AI tells customers about your products or services."

## 5. Alex's voice memos (13 Oct)

**The Media Engine (memo was machine-translated into German; meaning below)**
- Influx owns, runs and partners with pages in its ecosystem, like Instagram theme pages ("daily motivation" style). When a client's post goes live, those pages engage with it, which raises reach, distribution and impressions.
- "Something hardly anyone has."
- **Framing warning from Alex:** they have to be careful, because in theory it isn't a practice LinkedIn supports. Publicly they only say "we have a media engine we use to drive distribution."
- Results come from strategy + posting frequency + the Media Engine together.

**Why founders who already post still get nothing**
- "Posting and hoping" instead of engineering it: text dumps with no line-by-line formatting or spacing, AI slop, wrong length, wrong topics, not knowing what's being talked about right now.
- "More than anything else": companies in our ICP that post a lot aren't creating original content. They repost the company page and post "pure bottom of funnel stuff. Like, hey, we just released this new feature. Hey, speak to our team. Hey, go and visit our website."
- "Sometimes they think, hey, I'm posting a lot. But I'm not getting any success… because they're not taking prospects on a journey. They're not nurturing prospects."

## 6. What changes for the ads
- **Never describe the network engaging with posts** ("our pages like your post", "within 15 minutes they engage"). Alex's words: be careful with framing. Say "we have our own distribution on LinkedIn" at most.
- **1.2's reason is now documented:** they post, but it's reposts and pitches, and nobody gets taken from "never heard of you" to "ready to talk". Backed by Alex, both case studies, Tony and Semrush.
- **New angle for AG2:** "you're still the rainmaker; your team can manage accounts but can't open new doors" (Tony).
- **1.3 has a real story:** a marketing hire who handled LinkedIn, the messaging was off, and the founder had no time to manage it (Tony / Alyssa).
